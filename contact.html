Looking Ahead

Tennis has shaped the way I approach different challenges, leadership, and my personal growth. The sport has taught me how to stay and remain focused, work hard, and keep going even when things are difficult.

After graduation, I plan to continue building my education and career goals. Over the summer, I'll be working as a tennis pro in East Hamptons, New York. I'm really looking forward to the opportunity of teaching kids and adults how to play the sport that I fell in love with. I am interested in business, marketing and data analytics, and I hope to use the discipline I gained from tennis in the next stage of my life. In the near future, I'll be attending and completing a MBA program.

My tennis journey has given me skills that go beyond the court, including confidence, communication, teamwork, and resilience.
