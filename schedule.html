Life Around Tennis

Tennis was not just something I did after school. For most of my life, it shaped my schedule, my weekends, my travel, and sometimes even when I could relax. My day job was tennis.

Junior Tennis

During junior tennis, I trained for long hours each week while balancing school, travel, and tournaments. My training varied on how busy the junior tennis season was, but I trained anywhere from 25-50 hours per week. Some weeks felt like tennis, homework, food, sleep, and repeat.

Traveling for tournaments meant early mornings, packed bags, and lots of snacks. It was tiring, but it also created some of my favorite memories. For out of state tournaments, that meant catching a flight with my family or coach at 6 am usually.

College tennis added another level of responsibility. Practices, lifts, matches, classes, and travel made time management one of the biggest skills I learned. In season, training was 20 hours of hitting a week and 3 to 5 hours of lift, not including matches. And 8 am lift was brutal at times. As a student-athlete, its important to coordinate with faculty, your athletic academic advisor, trainers, and athletic staff to ensure you're on pace and healthy in all aspects.
